This repository holds the Altium design for the ALCT Spartan-6 LX150T
mezzanine, produced for the CSC Phase 2 Upgrade. The documentation and
design files below are archived from the CMS TWiki topic
[[https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/view/CMS/ALCTCSCPhase2Upgrade][ALCTCSCPhase2Upgrade]].

Slides from the ALCT mezzanine Production Readiness Review, held on 8 June
2018 ([[https://indico.cern.ch/event/734723/][indico]]), provide additional information:
[[file:assets/20180608_alct_mezzanines_prr.pdf][20180608_alct_mezzanines_prr.pdf]]. The committee conclusions from the review
are available here: [[file:assets/ALCT_MEZ_Review_w_append.pdf][ALCT_MEZ_Review_w_append.pdf]].

Below, Version 1 refers to prototypes, Version 2 has been used for the
production runs.

* Overview

The ALCT Spartan-6 LX150T mezzanine is designed as a replacement mezzanine
for ALCT-672 motherboards, intended to be used in ME2/1, ME3/1, and ME4/1.
It also retains compatibility for ALCT-288 and ALCT-384 motherboards, but
its use is not expected there.

Relative to the 2012 Spartan-6 mezzanine, the new LX150T mezzanine adds two
3.2 Gbps (line rate) optical links providing 5.12 Gbps of user DAQ
bandwidth to the ODMB on a CERN radiation hard VTTx.

Due to the lack of LHC synchronous (40.08 MHz) reference clock on the board
because of a scarcity of QPLL ASICs, a commercial asynchronous 80.0 MHz
oscillator is used for the MGT reference clock.

* Radiation testing

For 3000 fb^-1 of HL-LHC running, the maximum expected TID for the
ALCT-LX150T is 3 kRad.

All parts used on the ALCT-LX150T ([[file:assets/Bill_of_Materials-ALCT_MEZ_LX150T.csv][BOM]]) have been tested with TIDs equal or
greater to 30 kRad. Slides 62-64 of the ALCT talk in the ESR
([[file:assets/18-07-02_Andrew_Peck_-_alct_mezzanines_esr_v6.pdf][pdf]]) lay out the tests for each part:

- Most parts were also used in the ALCT-S6 board, installed during LS1.
  These parts were successfully tested in June 2012 at the UC Davis
  cyclotron to 30 kRad ([[file:assets/12-07-20_Jay_Hauser_-_ALCT_mezz_for_ME11-2.pdf][pdf]])
- The new XC6SLX150T FPGA is also used in the MPC and was successfully
  tested in April 2013 at the UC Davis cyclotron to 100 kRad
  ([[file:assets/13-04-01_Davis_LX150T_MPC_tests.pdf][pdf]]). MGTs were successfully tested in Jun 2018 to 15 kRad
  ([[file:assets/18-07-25_TAMU_test_Feb_2017.pdf][pdf]])
- The new oscillators (ASV-33.000MHZ-EJ-T and SG3225VAN 80.000000M-KEGA3)
  were tested in April 2018 at the Texas A&M reactor to 30 kRad
  ([[file:assets/18-07-21_TAMU_test_Mar_2018.pdf][pdf]])
- The VTTx is a radiation-hard transceiver qualified by CERN
  ([[file:assets/VTRx_Spec_v2.2.pdf][pdf]])
